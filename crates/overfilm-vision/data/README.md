# Built-in screen templates

`owcs-korea-2026.overfilm-screens.json`: the screen templates and player bars learnt (with
`overfilm.screenTemplate.learn` / `overfilm.playerBar.learn`) from frames of an OWCS Asia Korea
2026 broadcast at 720p: the break screen's left panel, the "PREPARE TO ATTACK", "PREPARE YOUR
DEFENSES", "GET READY" and "ASSEMBLE YOUR HEROES" banners, the in-game score bar, and both teams'
name plates. Gray grids of at most 128 cells per rectangle, not images.

OverFilm is a private, personal build (see `OVERFILM.md`): these are built in at the owner's
request so Auto Cut and the kill scan work on that league's broadcasts out of the box. They
derive from the league's broadcast graphics and the game's HUD; do not ship this file in a public
release.
